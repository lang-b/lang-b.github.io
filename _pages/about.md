Hi there! Welcome to my page. I'm a current Master's student at <a href="https://adelaide.edu.au/">Adelaide University</a>, under the supervison of <a href="https://roughan.info/about/">Prof Matthew Roughan</a>, <a href="https://researchers.adelaide.edu.au/profile/ben.ye">Dr Ben Ye</a>, and <a href="https://researchers.adelaide.edu.au/profile/hung.nguyen">Prof Hung Nguyen</a>. My research sits within the realms of applied math, network science and data science. My Master's research specifically focuses on Australia's Internet: investigating the structure, resilience, and diversity of the network, such that the network can withstand failures and disruptions.

I completed my undergrad in Mechanical & Aerospace Engineering, with a double degree in Mathematics, finishing in 2022. Outside of academics, I play music, soccer, and run marathons. If any of our research interests align, or you just want to chat, feel free to reach out!
